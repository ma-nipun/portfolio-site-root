# What two years of production teaches you

The boring, repeatable habits that keep a real application running and users unbothered.

## Core Lessons

1. **Boring is better:** Standard setups beat complex custom frameworks when debugging at 2 AM.
2. **Log what matters:** Structure your logs so key events can be queried immediately.
3. **Test recovery early:** Backups only count if you have tried restoring them.
