# Adding sane monitoring to the homelab

A small stack for seeing what is healthy before something becomes a late-night mystery.

## The goal

Monitoring should answer three questions quickly: **is it working, what changed, and what should I do next?** For a home lab, that means useful signals without building a second job to maintain the monitoring system.

## The stack

- **Uptime Kuma** for simple HTTP and TCP checks.
- **Grafana** for the few charts that are actually useful.
- **Node Exporter** for host-level CPU, memory, disk, and network metrics.

The important part is not the tool list. It is keeping the dashboards small enough that I will actually look at them.

## What I learned

Start with failure detection, then add context. A green dashboard is not proof that the system is healthy, but a clear alert with a useful link is a great place to begin debugging.
